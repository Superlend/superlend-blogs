**Loop Vaults** are automated DeFi strategies designed to maximize yield while keeping the process simple.

Whether you prefer a fully hands-off experience or want to fine-tune your strategy, Loop Vaults and Loop Markets put you in control, with the potential to earn more than traditional supply-only lending. 

### What Are Loop Vaults?

Loop Vaults are smart vaults that work to grow your deposits by using a looping strategy \- borrowing and re-depositing to increase exposure and amplify returns.

* ***You** deposit one token (the vault’s base token).*  
* ***The vault** handles the rest in the background.*

### Here’s how it works:

1. Deposit the vault’s base token (e.g., XTZ) or another supported token, which will be swapped for you.

2. Receive vault shares, representing your share of the vault’s total assets.

3. The vault can **“**loop” your position by borrowing and reinvesting to boost potential yield.

4. Withdraw anytime \- instantly if there’s enough liquidity, or schedule a withdrawal if funds are in active strategies.

### Safety & Withdrawals

Loop Vaults are built with user control and transparency in mind:

* Instant withdrawals – Available when the vault has enough liquidity.

* Scheduled withdrawals – If funds are in active loops, place a request and the vault will unwind to free them up.

* Built-in safeguards – All strategies are [audited](https://docs.superlend.xyz/superlend-loop/loop-strategy-vaults/security) and follow risk parameters to avoid unsafe leverage.
